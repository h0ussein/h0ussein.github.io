Mob Siege

Hold to fire blue soldiers from your cannon, steer them through the number doors to multiply your army, smash the red forts and beat the warlords of the Red Horde. One finger is all you need.

Free to play, works offline. Rewarded video ads are always your choice; a short ad may appear after a win. The game keeps your progress on your phone only: no account, no sign-up. Children may play: the game asks your year of birth once, only to choose the right kind of ads, and keeps the answer on your phone.
Google Play: coming soon.

Where it is available

Mob Siege is not offered in the European Union, the United Kingdom or South Korea.
