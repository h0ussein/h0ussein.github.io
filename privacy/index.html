Privacy Policy — Mob Siege

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Introduction

Mob Siege is a free mobile game. This policy explains what information the game and its advertising partner collect, how it is used, and what you can do about it. The short version: your game progress stays on your device, the game has no accounts and no login, and the developer does not sell your data.

Information we collect

Your game progress. Your level, coins, upgrades and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us.

No accounts. The game has no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and we do not collect any information you type into the game.

Advertising data. The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use the device's advertising ID and may collect:

  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location, derived from the IP address
  • Interactions with ads
  • Crash and diagnostic data

This data is collected by Google, not by us directly. For players under 13 (or who prefer not to say their age) the advertising ID is not used: see "Children" below.

How it is used

The advertising data is used to show ads, to measure the performance of ads, and to prevent fraud and abuse of the ad system. We use it for these purposes only.

Advertising (AdMob)

The game uses the Google AdMob SDK and shows two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads: you choose to watch one, for example to get a coin multiplier on the loot meter after a win.
  • Interstitial ads: shown now and then after a win (not in your first minutes, and never more than once every 90 seconds).

Rewarded ads are always your choice. Interstitial ads can appear after a win, as described above. With no internet connection the game shows no ads. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. We do not sell your data.

Google's own privacy policy applies to ad data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent (where the law asks for it)

Where the law asks for it (for example in Switzerland or some US states), Google shows you its consent form (the User Messaging Platform, or UMP) before any ad is requested (it is not shown to children: see "Children" below). You use that form to choose whether ads are personalized and you can change your choice at any time in the game under SETTINGS > PRIVACY OPTIONS. If you decline, ads are shown without personalization or not at all, as the law requires. The game is not offered in the European Union, the United Kingdom or South Korea.

Children

Mob Siege is a game for a general audience, and children may play it. When you first open the game it asks for your year of birth. The answer stays on your phone: it is never sent to us or to anyone else, and SETTINGS > RESET PROGRESS erases it. It is used for one thing only: to choose the right kind of ads.

  • Under 13, or if you prefer not to say: the game treats you as a child. Ads are requested with Google's "tag for child-directed treatment" and the content rating G (suitable for all ages). These ads are not personalized, and the game does not send the advertising ID of your device with them. The consent form described above is not shown to children.
  • 13 and older: the normal flow described above: the consent form where the law asks for it, and personalized ads only if you agree.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no accounts, no chat and no way to type in personal details, and the only company whose software talks to the internet in the game is Google (AdMob).

Data retention and deletion

Because the game has no accounts, there is no account data for us to delete. Your progress is removed from the device when you uninstall the game, or when you clear the game's data in your phone settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings at any time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or the ad services change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. If a change is significant, the game will tell you when you open it.
